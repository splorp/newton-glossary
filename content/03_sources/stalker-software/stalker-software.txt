Title: Stalker Software

----

Date: 05 September 2024

----

Text:

A developer of messaging, networking, and communication software for Mac OS and other platforms. Founded in Cologne, Germany in 1991, the company changed its name to CommuniGate Systems, a riff on its flagship product, the CommuniGate Pro communication server.
