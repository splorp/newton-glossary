Title: Sarofax

----

Date: 02 August 2026

----

Text:

A company formed by former (link: /sources/sign-of-the-times text: Sign of the Times) co-founder Ash Mishra with a focus on developing solutions using the [WebObjects](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WebObjects) application framework. In 1999, Sarofax acquired most of the software developed by Sine of the Times, releasing it as “charityware”. In 2004, Mishra released the software as open source.
