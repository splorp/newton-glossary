Title: Q

----

Date: 12 June 2001

----

Text:

Code name of the (link: /terms/messagepad-2000 text: MessagePad 2000) and (link: /terms/messagepad-2100 text: MessagePad 2100) devices.

The front of the (link: /terms/prototype text: prototype) devices were labeled either (link: /terms/q-system text: Q System) or (link: /terms/evt text: EVT), depending on the development stage.

Q is also the code name of the rumoured (link: /terms/messagepad-150 text: MessagePad 150) according to the (link: /sources/mackido text: Mackido) website.
