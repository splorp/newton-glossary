Title: MSNUG

----

Date: 24 August 2016

----

Text:

Michigan State Newton Users Group

Originally founded by [Douglas B. Luckie](https://web.archive.org/web/20190921033332/http://msu.edu/~luckie/dluckie.html) in April 1997, this user group became a (link: /terms/special-interest-group text: special interest group) (Newton SIG) within the (link: /terms/mspug text: MSPUG) (Michigan State Palmtop Users Group) in April 1998, after the Newton was [discontinued](https://web.archive.org/web/20040528183253/http://www.apple.com/pr/library/1998/feb/27newton.html).
