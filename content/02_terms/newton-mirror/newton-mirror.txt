Title: Newton Mirror

----

Date: 06 August 2026

----

Text:

A (link: /terms/newton-os text: Newton OS) version of the infamous Palm Mirror (link: /terms/application text: application) developed by [Bill Westerman](https://utilware.com/) for Palm OS. Like the original, (link: /sources/jim-anderson text: Jim Anderson)’s Newton Mirror changes the (link: /terms/screen text: screen) into a reflective black surface, effectively turning a Newton device into a handheld mirror.

Also referred to as (link: /terms/newtmirror text: NewtMirror).
