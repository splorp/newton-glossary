Title: UKNewt

----

Date: 19 October 2026

----

Text:

A non-profit organization formed in 2001 by Garry Whittaker and Bob Barkany.

Officially known as “The Independent Association for UK Newton Owners”, UKNewt provided an independent voice for Newton users in the United Kingdom, specifically as an alternative to the (link: /terms/assist text: Assist) user group organized by [Apple](https://www.apple.com/).
