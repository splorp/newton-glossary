Text:

A Newton device rumoured to have been in development around the same time as the (link: /terms/MessagePad 150 text: MessagePad 150), the purported sucessor to the (link: /terms/messagepad-120 text: MessagePad 120).

The <a href="https://www.panasonic.com/">Panasonic</a> Slate was to have featured a larger form factor and a “VGA-sized” (link: /terms/display text: display).

A (link: /terms/tablet-computer text: tablet computer) can also be referred to as a slate.
