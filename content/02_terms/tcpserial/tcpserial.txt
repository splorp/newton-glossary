Title: TCPSerial

----

Date: 05 September 2026

----

Text:

Software developed at the University of Michigan that simulates a (link: /terms/serial text: serial) driver and (link: /terms/modem text: modem) using a (link: /terms/tcp-ip text: TCP/IP) connection on a Mac OS computer.

TCPSerial is useful in situations where an (link: /terms/application text: application) requires an active serial port to work correctly, such as (link: /terms/newton-toolkit text: Newton Toolkit) running in the Classic environment on Mac OS X.
