Title: Sharp

----

Date: 19 September 2026

----

Text:

An (link: /terms/application text: application) located in the (link: /terms/extras-drawer text: Extras Drawer) used for exchanging information between a Newton device and certain [Sharp Wizard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharp_Wizard) electronic organizers.

(image: /assets/images/sharp-2x.gif alt: Screenshot of the Sharp application width: 212)

The Sharp application was only available on devices running (link: /terms/Newton-OS text: Newton OS) 1.x.

In prerelease versions of Newton OS 1.0, the (link: /terms/help-browser text: help browser) refers to the Sharp application as (link: /terms/wizard text: Wizard).

Sharp also refers to the (link: /sources/sharp text: Sharp Corporation).
