Title: keySound

----

Date: 19 September 2026

----

Text:

In (link: /terms/newtonscript text: NewtonScript), a (link: /terms/slot text: slot) that defines the (link: /terms/pen-sound-effects text: pen sound effect) when the user (link: /terms/tap text: taps) a key on an onscreen (link: /terms/keyboard text: keyboard).

The default sound for this effect is called (link: /terms/typewriter text: Typewriter).

Prerelease versions of (link: /terms/newton-os text: Newton OS) 1.0 also refer to the default sound as keySound.
