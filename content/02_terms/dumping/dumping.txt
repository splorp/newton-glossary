Title: Dumping

----

Date: 06 August 2026

----

Text:

A colloquial term describing the process of exporting (link: /terms/text text: text) or data from a Newton device.

For example, exporting a (link: /terms/soup text: soup) using Steve Weyer’s (link: /terms/sloup text: Sloup) utility by executing the `DUMP!` command is referred to as dumping.
