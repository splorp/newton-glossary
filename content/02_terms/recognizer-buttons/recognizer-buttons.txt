Title: Recognizer Buttons

----

Date: 21 September 2026

----

Text:

A pair of (link: /terms/picture-button text: picture buttons) located in the (link: /terms/status-bar text: status bar) in early versions of (link: /terms/newton-os text: Newton OS) that allow the user to select the method of (link: /terms/handwriting-recognition text: handwriting recognition) used to interpret what is being written or drawn.

(image: /assets/images/recognizer-buttons-2x.gif alt: Screenshot of the Text Recognizer Toggle and Recognition Buttons in the Status Bar width: 240)

The recognizer buttons were replaced in Newton OS 2.0 with a single (link: /terms/recognition-button text: recognition button) that displays a (link: /terms/recognizer-picker text: recognizer picker) when (link: /terms/tapped text: tapped).

In prerelease versions of Newton OS 1.0, the recognizer buttons are referred to as (link: /terms/interpreter-buttons text: interpreter buttons).
