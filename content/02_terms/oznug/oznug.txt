Australian Newton Users Group

Founded in August 1994, OzNUG was based in Sydney and supported subchapters of the group throughout Australia, including (link: /terms/boznug text: bOzNUG) in Brisbane, (link: /terms/moznug text: mOzNUG) in Melbourne, (link: /terms/soznug text: sOzNUG) in Adelaide, and (link: /terms/woznug text: wOzNUG) in Perth.

The user group published a regular newsletter called (link: /terms/scribbles text: Scribbles).

OzNUG was also affiliated with (link: /terms/nanug text: NANUG), a very active (link: /terms/newton-user-group text: Newton user group) in the United States.
