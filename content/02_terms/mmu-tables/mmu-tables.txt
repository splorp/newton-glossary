Title: MMU Tables

----

Date: 31 May 2001

----

Text:

A structured set of data used by the memory management unit ((link: /terms/mmu text: MMU)) which instructs it on how to handle the translation of (link: /terms/virtual-address text: virtual addresses) to (link: /terms/absolute-address text: absolute addresses).
