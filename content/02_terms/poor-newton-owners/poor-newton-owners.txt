Philadelphia Organization of Responsible Newton Owners

A user group based in the Philadelphia, Pennsylvania area.

Organized by Lawrence Husick, an intellectual property and technology law consultant, the group’s name is a tip of the [tricorne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tricorne) to _Poor Richard’s Almanack_, first published in 1733 by Benjamin Franklin.
