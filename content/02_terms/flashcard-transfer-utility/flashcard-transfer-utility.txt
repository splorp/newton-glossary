Title: flashCard Transfer Utility

----

Date: 14 August 2026

----

Text:

An (link: /terms/application text: application) developed by (link: /terms/powerpen text: powerPen) that allows importing and exporting (link: /terms/deck text: decks) containing question and answer data used by (link: /terms/flashcard text: flashCard). The software transfers information from a Newton device over a (link: /terms/serial text: serial) connection to another computer running a terminal emulator.

(image: /assets/images/flashcard-transfer-utility-2x.gif alt: Screenshot of the flashCard Transfer Utility slip width: 216)

Also referred to as (link: /terms/fcx text: FCX), (link: /terms/ftu text: FTU), (link: /terms/fcxfer text: fCXfer), and (link: /terms/fc-transfer-utility text: fC Transfer Utility).
