Title: NewtChamp

----

Date: 25 September 2026

----

Text:

A user group based in Vermont, organized by Hardy Macia of (link: /sources/catamount text: Catamount Software). Originally formed as a (link: /terms/special-interest-group text: special interest group) (SIG) within the [MacChamp](https://web.archive.org/web/19970616064854/http://www.sover.net/~gm7234/macintosh.html) Macintosh user group, NewtChamp held their first meeting in November 1995, disbanding in 1998.
