Title: powerPen

----

Date: 12 August 2026

----

Text:

A student-run organization at [Washington State University](https://wsu.edu/) that developed software for the (link: /terms/newton-os text: Newton OS) platform.

Originally a computer engineering course called “Team-Oriented Software Practicum”, it supplemented traditional course-based fundamentals with an ongoing practical curriculum modelled after the activities of a software company.

Instigated by faculty advisor (link: /sources/jeffrey-schlimmer text: Jeffrey C. Schlimmer) in 1994, the group was dissolved in 1997 after Schlimmer’s departure from the school.

Software released by powerPen included (link: /terms/quickport text: QuickPort), flashCard, hangMan, powerNotes, and powerBack.
