Title: NewtPlay

----

Date: 03 November 2026

----

Text:

A utility developed by (link: /sources/matthias-melcher text: Matthias Melcher) that allows the user to launch and run a Newton (link: /terms/package text: package) as a native desktop (link: /terms/application text: application) on macOS.

Created as a proof of concept, NewtPlay incorporates a very limited subset of (link: /terms/newtonscript text: NewtonScript) supplied by the (link: /terms/newtc text: newtc) component of (link: /terms/vsnewt text: VSNewt), a (link: /terms/compiler text: compiler), runtime, and (link: /terms/debugger text: debugger) is based on Simon Bell’s (link: /terms/newton-framework text: Newton Framework).
