----

Date: 06 October 2002

Text:

Los Angeles Newton Users Group

Originally founded by [Phil Goodman](https://www.goodmanconsulting.com/about/) as a (link: /terms/special-interest-group text: special interest group) within the [Los Angeles Macintosh Group](http://www.lamg.org/) (LAMG), LANUG was eventually folded into the Los Angeles Portable Computing Group (LAPCG), which in turn became the [Los Angeles Portable Users Group](https://www.lapug.com/) (LAPUG) in 1992.
