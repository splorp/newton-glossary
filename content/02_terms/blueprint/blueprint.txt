Title: BluePrint

----

Date: 21 September 2026

----

Text:

A project manager (link: /terms/application text: application) developed by (link: /sources/sign-of-the-times text: Sign of the Times) used for planning and organizing complex activities, including (link: /terms/task text: tasks) and (link: /terms/meeting text: meetings).

(image: /assets/images/blueprint-2x.gif alt: Screenshot of the BluePrint application width: 320)

BluePrint also facilitates transferring [Microsoft Project](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Project) files from a Mac OS or Windows computer to a Newton device.
