Title: NewtonMail Modules

----

Date: 29 July 2026

----

Text:

Software provided by (link: /terms/apple-online-services text: Apple Online Services) (AOS) to enhance and extend the functionality and usability of (link: /terms/newtonmail text: NewtonMail) on a Newton device.

Supplied as a set of (link: /terms/package text: packages), the modules allow the user to copy of text from a message to the (link: /terms/notepad text: NotePad) application, easily reply to and forward messages, send messages to multiple recipients, print and fax directly from the (link: /terms/inout-box text: In/Out Box), and access NewtonMail through the Tymnet/MCI network.
