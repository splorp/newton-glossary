Title: eWorld

----

Date: 30 October 2001

----

Text:

Apple’s one time foray into the realm of consumer online services. Based on the same operational platform as America Online, eWorld offered a wealth of electronic publications and services targeted mainly at [Apple](https://www.apple.com/) customers. The service was only available on the Mac OS and Apple IIGS platforms, along with limited support on (link: /terms/newton-os text: Newton OS) using special email (link: /terms/stationery text: stationery).

The operation and support of eWorld was the responsibility of the (link: /terms/apple-online-services text: Apple Online Services) (AOS) division.
