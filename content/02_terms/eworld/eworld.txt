Title: eWorld

----

Date: 30 October 2001

----

Text:

Apple’s one time foray into the realm of consumer online services. Based on the same operational platform as America Online, eWorld offered a wealth of electronic publications and services targeted mainly at [Apple](https://www.apple.com/) customers. The service was only available on the Mac OS platform, along with limited support on (link: /terms/newton-os text: Newton OS) using special email (link: /terms/stationery text: stationery).
