Title: USB

----

Date: 24 May 2001

Text:

Universal Serial Bus

A hardware interface standard for low speed, (link: /terms/serial text: serial) peripherals such as keyboards, mice, and other (link: /terms/input text: input) devices. USB devices can be hot swapped.
