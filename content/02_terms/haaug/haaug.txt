Text:

Houston Area Apple Users Group

A user group based in Houston, Texas that was founded in 1978. Although more generally focused on [Apple](https://www.apple.com/) and Mac OS platform, HAAUG also maintained an active Newton (link: /terms/special-interest-group text: special interest group) or (link: /terms/newton-sig text: Newton SIG) until 2002.
