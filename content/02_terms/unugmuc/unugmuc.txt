Text:

Unabhängige Newton User Group München

A user group founded by (link: /sources/nick-muller text: Nick Müller) in Munich, Germany.

After the Newton was [discontinued](https://web.archive.org/web/20040528183253/http://www.apple.com/pr/library/1998/feb/27newton.html), the group transitioned to the Unabhängige Mac User Group Munich (UMUGMUC).

Also referred to as (link: /terms/newton-stammtisch text: Newton-Stammtisch) and (link: /terms/newton-mac-stammtisch text: Newton-Mac-Stammtisch).

Stammtisch translates to “Regulars’ Table”, referring to a group of people who meet regularly at the same location.
