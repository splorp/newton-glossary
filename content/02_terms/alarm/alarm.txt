Title: Alarm

----

Date: 23 January 2025

----

Text:

A section found in the (link: /terms/prefs text: Prefs) application.

In the Alarm (link: /terms/slip text: slip), the user can set the type of sound used for the minute timer and (link: /terms/calendar text: calendar) alarms. The daily alarm has its own “(link: /terms/alarm-wakeup text: alarm wakeup)” sound and cannot be changed. The user can also specify other settings such as the (link: /terms/volume text: volume) for all alarms, whether the system volume overrides the alarm volume, and whether (link: /terms/persistent-alarms text: persistent alarms) are enabled.

(image: /assets/images/alarm-2x.gif alt: Screenshot of the Alarm preference slip width: 240)

In (link: /terms/newton-os text: Newton OS) 1.x, (link: /terms/alarm-sound text: alarm sounds) are referred to as an (link: /terms/alarm-sound-effects text: alarm sound effects) and are set in the (link: /terms/sound text: Sound) section of the Prefs application.
