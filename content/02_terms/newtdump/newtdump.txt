Title: NewtDump

----

Date: 24 May 2001

----

Text:

A Windows-based (link: /terms/application text: application) developed by (link: /sources/victor-rehorst text: Victor Rehorst) that works with Steve Weyer’s (link: /terms/sloup text: Sloup) utility to export (or (link: /terms/dump text: dump)) data from a Newton device.

(image: /assets/images/newtdump-2x.gif alt: Screenshot of the NewtDump window width: 312)

NewtDump supports exporting (link: /terms/inout-box text: InOut Box) messages and (link: /terms/notes text: Notepad) entries as (link: /terms/text text: text), (link: /terms/name-file text: Name File) items as text and (link: /terms/vcard text: vCard), as well as (link: /terms/date-book text: Date Book) events.
