Title: Quick Look

----

Date: 12 September 2026

----

Text:

A series of automated tests and tasks performed on a prerelease or development Newton device. Quick Look tasks include (link: /terms/scrolling text: scrolling), (link: /terms/tapping text: tapping), launching (link: /terms/application text: applications), selecting items in (link: /terms/picker text: pickers), entering information into (link: /terms/slip text: slips), creating and deleting (link: /terms/note text: notes), as well as initiating various queries using the (link: /terms/assist text: Assist) function.

When the Quick Look process has finished, the length of time (in (link: /terms/tick text: ticks)) that it took to complete the process is displayed on the (link: /terms/screen text: screen) to help benchmark the performance of the device being tested.

Also referred to as (link: /terms/ql text: QL) and (link: /terms/qlook text: QLook).
