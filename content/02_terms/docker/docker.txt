Title: Docker

----

Date: 19 September 2026

----

Text:

In (link: /terms/newtonscript text: NewtonScript), docker is a reference to the programmatic mechanism used when connecting or (link: /terms/docking text: docking) a Newton device to another computer.

The (link: /terms/help-browser text: help browser) in prerelease versions of (link: /terms/newton-os text: Newton OS) 1.0 refers to the (link: /terms/computer text: Computer) application as (link: /terms/docker text: Docker).
