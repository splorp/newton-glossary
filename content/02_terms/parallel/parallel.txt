Title: Parallel

----

Date: 04 September 2026

----

Text:

The transmission of data over multiple wires or communication channels one bit at a time, thereby multiplying the rate of data transfer.

Compare with (link: /terms/serial text: serial) transmission, where only a single channel is used.
