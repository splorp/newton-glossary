Boston Computer Society Newton Programmers Collective

An offshoot of (link: /terms/bcsng text: BCSNG), the Boston Computer Society Newton Group, that was formed to “facilitate communication and cooperation among the Newton
developers community”. Meetings included seminars on various aspects of (link: /terms/newton-os text: Newton OS) programming, software and hardware demonstrations, as well as technical question and answer sessions.
