Title: ARMadillo

----

Date: 15 October 2017

----

Text:

Reportedly, the last version of the NuBus-based Newton development card produced by [Apple](https://www.apple.com/) to facilitate the running of a Newton development environment on a Mac OS (link: /terms/desktop-computer text: desktop computer).

The ARMadillo card was preceded by the (link: /terms/armageddon text: ARMageddon) and (link: /terms/armistice text: ARMistice) cards. The cards were so named because of the (link: /terms/arm text: ARM) processor used in the Newton hardware.
