Title: Dumped

----

Date: 06 August 2026

----

Text:

A colloquial term describing (link: /terms/text text: text) or data that has been exported from a Newton device.

For example, a (link: /terms/soup text: soup) that has been exported to a (link: /terms/text text: text) file using Steve Weyer’s (link: /terms/sloup text: Sloup) utility by executing the `DUMP!` command is referred to as dumped.
