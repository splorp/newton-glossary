----

Date: 25 October 2026

Text:

An open source software project instigated by (link: /sources/daniel-parnell text: Daniel Parnell) to reverse engineer the (link: /terms/newton-os text: Newton OS) ROM.

The project specifically targets the (link: /terms/rom text: ROM) found in (link: /terms/n2 text: N2) devices such as the (link: /terms/messagepad-2000 text: MessagePad 2000) and (link: /terms/messagepad-2100 text: MessagePad 2100), with the goal of creating a maintainable reimplementation of Newton OS running natively on other computing platforms.
