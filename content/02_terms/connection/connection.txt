Title: Connection

----

Date: 24 May 2001

----

Text:

The built-in (link: /terms/application text: application) used to perform the action of connecting or (link: /terms/docking text: docking) to another computer.

(image: /assets/images/connection-2x.gif alt: Screenshot of the Connection application width: 196)

In prerelease versions of (link: /terms/newton-os text: Newton OS) 1.0, the application is named (link: /terms/computer text: Computer).

In Newton OS 2.0 and later, the application is named (link: /terms/dock text: Dock).
