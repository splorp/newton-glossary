The Medical Students’ Guide to the Newton

A document created by Ron Risley while attending the [University of Florida](https://med.ufl.edu/) College of Medicine. Formatted as an (link: /terms/faq text: FAQ), the MSGN addressed common questions asked as a result of Risley carrying a Newton device around the hospital.
