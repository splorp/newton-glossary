Title: Unrelocation

----

Date: 30 July 2026

----

Text:

Paul Guyot’s colloquial term for using an email (link: /terms/transport text: transport) to exporting packages from a Newton device.

Unrelocation is essentially the opposite of the (link: /terms/relocation text: relocation) process, allowing a package to be deactivated and moved elsewhere.
