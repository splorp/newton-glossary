Title: Unrelocation

----

Date: 30 July 2026

----

Text:

A colloquial term coined by (link: /sources/paul-guyot text: Paul Guyot) referring to the use of an email (link: /terms/transport text: transport) to export a (link: /terms/package text: package) from a Newton device.

Unrelocation is essentially the opposite of the (link: /terms/relocation text: relocation) process, allowing a package to be deactivated and moved elsewhere.
