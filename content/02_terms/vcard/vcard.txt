Title: vCard

----

Date: 24 May 2001

----

Text:

A standardized data format for electronic business cards. Information is typically contained in a file identified by the (link: /terms/vcf text: .vcf) filename extension, which can be exchanged via email, instant messaging, or other file transfer mechanism.

A vCard that has been transferred to a Newton device can be converted into a (link: /terms/name-file text: Name File) record using an (link: /terms/application text: application) such as (link: /terms/simplemail text: SimpleMail) and (link: /terms/mail-v text: Mail V).
