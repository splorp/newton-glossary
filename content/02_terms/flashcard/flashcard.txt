Title: flashCard

----

Date: 14 August 2026

----

Text:

An (link: /terms/application text: application) developed by (link: /terms/powerpen text: powerPen) as a memorization tool for test preparation in education. Using a simple question and answer format, text-based “(link: /terms/deck text: decks)” could be created on another computer and imported to a Newton device using the (link: /terms/flashcard-transfer-utility text: flashCard Transfer Utility). flashCard decks created on a Newton device could also be exported to a computer for editing, archiving, or distributing to other users.

Also referred to as (link: /terms/fc text: fC).

Not to be confused with a (link: /terms/flash-card text: Flash Card).
