Title: Deck

----

Date: 12 August 2026

----

Text:

A set of questions and answers used by (link: /terms/flashcard text: FlashCard). A deck can be created on a Newton device within the FlashCard (link: /terms/application text: application) or on another computer in the form of a lightly formatted (link: /terms/text text: text) file. A deck can be imported into FlashCard using the (link: /terms/flashcard-transfer-utility text: FlashCard Transfer Utility).
