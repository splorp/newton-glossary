Title: Dump

----

Date: 06 August 2026

----

Text:

To export text or data from a Newton device.

`DUMP!` is also command that instructs Steve Weyer’s (link: /terms/sloup text: Sloup) utility to export (link: /terms/text text: text) or data from a specified (link: /terms/soup text: soup) to a file located on a host computer.

The process of exporting data using the `DUMP!` command is referred to as (link: /terms/dumping text: dumping).
