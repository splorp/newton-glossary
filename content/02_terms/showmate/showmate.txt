Title: ShowMate

----

Date: 02 August 2026

----

Text:

An (link: /terms/infrared text: infrared) remote control (link: /terms/application text: application) developed by (link: /sources/sign-of-the-times text: Sign of the Times) that allows a Newton device to manage and control several types of audio and video equipment by creating (link: /terms/script text: scripts) to automate control functions.

(image: /assets/images/showmate-2x.gif alt: Screenshot of the ShowMate application width: 236)

Scripts created in ShowMate could be scheduled to run in sequence or at a certain time of the day by setting an (link: /terms/alarm text: alarm). The accompanying (link: /terms/myremote text: MyRemote) utility provided a way to create custom remote control (link: /terms/interface text: interfaces).
