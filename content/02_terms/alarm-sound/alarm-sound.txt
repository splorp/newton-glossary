Title: Alarm Sound

----

Date: 28 October 2026

----

Text:

A setting in (link: /terms/alarm text: Alarm) section of the (link: /terms/prefs text: Prefs) application that allows the user to set the type of sound used for the minute timer and (link: /terms/calendar text: calendar) alarms. The daily alarm has its own “(link: /terms/alarm-wakeup text: alarm wakeup)” sound and cannot be changed.

The (link: /terms/bell text: Bell), (link: /terms/chord text: Chord), (link: /terms/trill text: Trill), and (link: /terms/xylo text: Xylo) alarm sounds first appeared in Newton OS 1.0 and are available in all subsequent versions of the (link: /terms/operating-system text: operating system).

Newton OS 2.0 introduced additional sounds, including (link: /terms/birdie text: Birdie), (link: /terms/buzzer text: Buzzer), (link: /terms/chirp text: Chirp), (link: /terms/click text: Click), (link: /terms/do-re-mi text: Do Re Mi), (link: /terms/echo text: Echo), (link: /terms/gong text: Gong), (link: /terms/out-there text: Out There), (link: /terms/psssssst text: Psssssst), (link: /terms/spring text: Spring), (link: /terms/wheep text: Wheep), and (link: /terms/whoop text: Whoop).

On devices running (link: /terms/newton-os text: Newton OS) 1.x, alarm sounds are called (link: /terms/alarm-sound-effects text: alarm sound effects).
