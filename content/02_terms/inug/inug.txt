Text:

Italian Newton User Group

A user group created to evangelize the (link: /terms/newton-os text: Newton OS) platform and help grow the Newton community in Italy.

Indiana Newton User Group

A user group [proposed by Jim Lopez](https://newtontalk.net/archive/newtontalk/2002-March/2186.html) during a discussion on the NewtonTalk mailing list in March 2002. Mainly consisting of Newton users based in Indiana, the group maintained their own [Yahoo! Groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yahoo_Groups) mailing list. 

The Indiana Newton User Group was also referred to as (link: /terms/nugi text: NUGi) and (link: /terms/indynewton text: IndyNewton)).
