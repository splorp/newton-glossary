Title: Poutine

----

Date: 06 October 2002

----

Text:

A delicious regional dish originating in Quebec, consisting of French fried potatoes and squeaky cheese curds, covered in savoury brown gravy.

Poutine is also [an oddly recurrent theme](https://marc.info/?l=newtontalk&w=2&r=1&s=poutine&q=t) on the (link: /terms/newtontalk text: NewtonTalk) mailing list.
