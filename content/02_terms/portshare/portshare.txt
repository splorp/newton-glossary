Title: PortShare

----

Date: 05 September 2026

----

Text:

Software that allows (link: /terms/serial text: serial) devices to be shared between multiple Mac OS computers on a network.

PortShare is useful in situations where an (link: /terms/application text: application) requires an active serial port to work correctly, such as (link: /terms/newton-toolkit text: Newton Toolkit) running in the Classic environment on Mac OS X.
