Title: Clone

----

Date: 31 May 2001

----

Text:

A device manufactured by a third-party company using Newton technology licensed from [Apple](https://www.apple.com/).

Newton clones include the Sharp (link: /terms/expertpad text: ExpertPad), Motorola (link: /terms/marco text: Marco), Digital Ocean (link: /terms/seahorse text: Seahorse), Digital Ocean (link: /terms/tarpon text: Tarpon), Harris (link: /terms/access-device-2000 text: Access Device), Harris Dracon (link: /terms/supertech-2000 text: SuperTech), Schlumberger (link: /terms/watson text: Watson), and Siemens (link: /terms/notephone text: NotePhone).

In (link: /terms/newtonscript text: NewtonScript), `Clone` is a (link: /terms/method text: method) which duplicates and returns a copy of a (link: /terms/cursor text: cursor) that can be modified without affecting the original.
